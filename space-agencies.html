AstraForge: Space Agency Data

AstraForge uses public mission and launcher data from space agencies around the world. Each card lists the agency, the spacecraft and launch vehicles from that country in the app, and the agency's official website. Figures are approximate and rounded from published sources.

India

ISRO · Indian Space Research Organisation

Spacecraft: Chandrayaan-3, Chandrayaan-2 Orbiter, Mangalyaan (Mars Orbiter Mission), Aditya-L1, Cartosat-3, EOS-04 (RISAT-1A), GSAT-11, IRNSS-1I (NavIC), AstroSat

Launch vehicles: SSLV, PSLV-XL, GSLV Mk II, LVM3

Launch site: Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota

https://www.isro.gov.in/
Europe

ESA · European Space Agency

Spacecraft: Sentinel-2A, Galileo FOC, JUICE, Solar Orbiter, Gaia, Rosetta

Launch vehicles: Ariane 64, Ariane 62, Vega-C (Arianespace)

Launch site: Guiana Space Centre, Kourou

https://www.esa.int/
Japan

JAXA · Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency

Spacecraft: Hayabusa2, SLIM, Himawari-9, ALOS-2 (Daichi-2), Akatsuki

Launch vehicles: H3-24, H-IIA 202, Epsilon

Launch site: Tanegashima and Uchinoura space centres

https://global.jaxa.jp/
China

CNSA · China National Space Administration

Spacecraft: Chang’e 5, Chang’e 6, Chang’e 4 + Yutu-2, Tianwen-1, BeiDou-3, Fengyun-4A

Launch vehicles: Long March 5, Long March 3B, Long March 2F, Long March 7

Launch site: Wenchang, Xichang and Jiuquan launch centres

https://www.cnsa.gov.cn/english/
Russia

Roscosmos · State Space Corporation

Spacecraft: Luna-25, GLONASS-K, Spektr-RG, Meteor-M No.2, Elektro-L

Launch vehicles: Soyuz-2.1b, Angara A5, Proton-M

Launch site: Baikonur, Plesetsk and Vostochny cosmodromes

https://www.roscosmos.ru/
South Korea

KASA · Korea AeroSpace Administration

Spacecraft: Danuri (KPLO), KOMPSAT-3, GEO-KOMPSAT-2A

Launch vehicles: Nuri (KSLV-II)

Launch site: Naro Space Center

https://www.kasa.go.kr/
United Kingdom

UK Space Agency

Spacecraft: OneWeb satellite, NovaSAR-1, Inmarsat-6 F1, Skynet 5A

Launch vehicles: Launched on partner rockets

https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-space-agency
Israel

ISA · Israel Space Agency

Spacecraft: Beresheet, AMOS-17, VENµS

Launch vehicles: Shavit-2

Launch site: Palmachim Airbase

https://www.gov.il/en/departments/units/most_isa1
United Arab Emirates

UAE Space Agency

Spacecraft: Hope (Emirates Mars Mission), KhalifaSat

Launch vehicles: Launched on partner rockets

https://space.gov.ae/
United Arab Emirates

MBRSC · Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre

Spacecraft: Builder of the Hope Mars probe and KhalifaSat

https://www.mbrsc.ae/
United States vehicles (Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, Atlas V 551, SLS Block 1, Electron) use public data from NASA, SpaceX, United Launch Alliance and Rocket Lab. NASA data is listed separately. No agency endorses this project.
