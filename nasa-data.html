AstraForge: NASA Data and Resources

Every NASA dataset and resource used to build AstraForge, the space mission design simulator, and how each one is used in the app.

1. NASA 3D Resources

Official 3D spacecraft models shown in the spacecraft viewer and during flights: Hubble, James Webb Space Telescope, Juno, Landsat, Perseverance, Voyager, Parker Solar Probe and Rosetta.

  • NASA 3D Resources
  • NASA 3D Resources on GitHub

2. NASA Blue Marble imagery

The real ground colour around each of the 18 launch sites, such as Sriharikota, Kennedy, Baikonur and Kourou.

  • NASA Visible Earth: Blue Marble collection

3. NASA mission facts

Published masses, power, antenna sizes and orbits for the NASA spacecraft templates, plus Space Launch System dimensions.

  • Hubble Space Telescope
  • James Webb Space Telescope
  • Europa Clipper
  • Juno
  • Mars 2020 Perseverance
  • Parker Solar Probe
  • New Horizons
  • Voyager
  • Landsat 9
  • Space Launch System (SLS)

4. NASA Deep Space Network

Antenna sizes and the link-budget method behind the communications model (data rate, signal delay, data sent home per day).

  • DSN mission documents, including the Telecommunications Link Design Handbook (810-005)

5. NASA Technology Readiness Levels

The TRL 1–9 scale used to rate every propulsion and launch concept in the Future Tech Lab.

  • NASA Technology Readiness Levels

6. The challenge

The NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 challenge that AstraForge answers.
